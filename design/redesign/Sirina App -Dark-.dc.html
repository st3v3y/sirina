Overview
A discovery call with Acme Corp's operations team about their client onboarding process. The current workflow is manual and spreadsheet-driven, with approval delays cited as the main pain point.
Key points
Onboarding is tracked in a shared spreadsheet with no status visibility.
Approvals stall when a document waits on one person with no reminder.
Lost time per stalled client is estimated at roughly a week.
